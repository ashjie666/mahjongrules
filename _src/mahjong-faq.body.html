At a glance

Scope
Forty questions that come up in a first session
Format
Two or three sentences each, with a link to the full page
Order
Setup, discards, scoring, then regional rules

Setup and the wall

How many tiles are in a set?

136 without flowers, 144 with them, and 152 in an American set that includes eight Jokers. The wall is built from all of them.

How many tiles does each player start with?

Thirteen, except the dealer, who starts with fourteen because they take the extra tile and discard first. Taiwanese play starts with sixteen.

Who deals first?

The player seated east, who is the dealer and throws the dice to decide where the wall is broken.

Do you need a special table?

No. A flat surface with room for four walls is enough. An automatic table speeds up shuffling and wall building but changes no rule.

How many copies of each tile exist?

Four of every numbered tile, every wind and every dragon. That limit is why a kong is only four tiles, and why tile counting works at all.

Claims and discards

Can you claim a discard to finish a run?

Yes in Chinese, Hong Kong, Taiwanese and riichi play. No in American mahjong, and only once per hand in British play.

What is the order of priority when two players want the same tile?

Win first, then kong, then pung, then chow. If two players can win on the same tile, the nearest player anticlockwise takes it unless the table uses an all-pay rule.

Can I change my mind after claiming?

No. A claimed tile becomes a fixed set and cannot be returned or rearranged.

What happens if I claim a tile and then find I cannot use it?

It becomes an illegal claim. At most tables the tile goes back and you are not allowed to claim again that hand; in tournament play the penalty is stricter.

Do I have to declare a win out loud?

Yes. Silence is not a win. Say "mahjong" or the local equivalent, clearly, before the next player draws.

Winning hands

What shape does a winning hand have?

Four sets and a pair, with seven pairs and thirteen orphans as the special exceptions. Taiwanese play uses five sets and a pair.

Can four identical tiles count as two pairs?

In Chinese Official and riichi, yes, inside a seven-pairs hand. Those tiles cannot also score as a pung.

Is a kong one set or two?

One. That is why a kong requires a replacement tile: without it the hand would be one tile short of a complete shape.

Why was my complete hand refused?

Either it did not split into four sets and a pair, or it failed the style's minimum. Check the shape first, then the value.

Can you win on a tile you discarded earlier?

In riichi, no — that is furiten. In Chinese styles the restriction does not exist and the win is legal.

Scoring

How many fan do I need in Chinese Official?

Eight. A hand below eight fan cannot be declared and is treated as a false win.

Do flower tiles count toward the eight?

No. Each flower is worth 1 fan on its own, but flowers are excluded when checking the minimum.

Who pays when I win?

On a discard, the discarder pays the full value alone. On a self-draw, all three opponents pay.

What is the difference between fan and fu?

Fan counts named patterns; fu counts structure, such as whether a pung was concealed or claimed. Riichi uses both; Chinese Official uses fan alone.

How is a riichi hand scored?

Base points are fu multiplied by 2 to the power of han plus 2. From five han upward the score stops doubling and falls into fixed tiers, starting at mangan.

What is a false win?

A declared win that does not meet the minimum. In tournament play it carries a penalty; at casual tables it usually costs you the hand.

Riichi rules

What does riichi actually do?

It declares that you are closed and one tile from winning. You gain 1 han and a deposit of 1,000 points, and you may not change your wait.

Can I riichi after claiming a tile?

No. Any claim opens the hand permanently and riichi requires a fully closed hand.

Can I kong after declaring riichi?

Only a concealed kong, and only if it does not change your wait. An added kong is not allowed.

What is furiten?

Being one tile from winning but unable to win on a discard, usually because that tile is already in your own river.

How does dora work?

The tile immediately after the flipped indicator is dora, one han per copy. Ura dora is revealed under the indicator and only counts for a riichi hand.

Can I win with dora but no yaku?

No. Dora adds han but is not a yaku, and a hand with no yaku cannot be declared.

Regional rules

Do I have to drop a suit in Sichuan mahjong?

Yes, it is a hard requirement. You must discard every tile of one suit before you can win, and the hand continues after the first win.

How many tai do I need in Taiwanese mahjong?

Five. Flowers add one tai each and are collected as they are drawn rather than counted at the end.

How many sets in a Taiwanese hand?

Five sets and a pair, from sixteen tiles. This is the one structural difference between Taiwanese and every other common style.

Which winds are used in three-player mahjong?

Only east, south and west matter as winds. Japanese three-player play keeps the north tiles in the set but treats a drawn north as a bonus tile you set aside; many Chinese three-player games remove the north wind entirely.

Can Jokers in American mahjong be used as a pair?

No. A Joker may only stand in for a tile inside a group of three or more identical tiles, never for a pair or a single tile.

How many times can you chow in British mahjong?

Once per hand. Pung and kong remain unlimited, and kong replacements come from a reserved kong box at the end of the wall.

What does limit mean in British play?

A per-hand score cap agreed by the table. It is not the same as the rating figure used in European tournament play.

Table behaviour

Do I have to keep my discards in a line?

Yes, and in front of you. The river is shared information; scattering tiles is treated as hiding them.

Can I touch another player's tiles?

No. You may look at the river, but moving or straightening another player's discards is not acceptable at any table.

What if I claim a tile by mistake?

Say so immediately, before the next discard. Most clubs allow a retraction; if the next tile has been played, the claim usually stands and may be penalised.

Is it rude to take a long time over a discard?

Only if it is habitual. Most tables expect a decision within about thirty seconds, and silent slow play is more disruptive than a mistake.

Can I ask what a rule is after the hand has started?

Yes, and you should. A disputed rule is settled before the next hand, not by the player who benefits from it.

Quick answers

What should I learn first?

The turn sequence and the four-set shape. Everything else is a variation on those two things, and both are covered on the rules and winning-hands pages.

Which style is best for a first game?

Hong Kong mahjong, because it allows chow, has a low minimum and is the version most English-speaking households already play.
