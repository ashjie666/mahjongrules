Disclaimer

Last reviewed: 30 October 2026

Mahjong Rules is an independent educational site. The notes below describe its limits, so that nothing on it is mistaken for something it is not.

Not an official rulebook

What is described here is how a rule set is commonly played, not a regulation issued by an authority. Mahjong has no single governing body, and the organisations that do publish rules — for Chinese Official, riichi, American and British play — each maintain their own version. Where this site summarises a rule set, the organisation's own publication remains authoritative.

House rules vary widely, including within the same city. If your club plays a version that differs from a page here, your club is right for your table.

Rules change

Some rule sets are revised. The American card is replaced every year, tournament regulations are amended between seasons, and platform implementations change without announcement. Pages that depend on a versioned publication say so, and each page shows the date it was last reviewed. A page that was accurate when written may be out of date now.

Not gambling advice

Nothing on this site is advice on betting, stakes, money play or the legal status of gambling in any jurisdiction. Mahjong is played for money in some places and strictly for fun in others. Whether you may play for stakes, and under what conditions, is a question for your local law and for your own judgement. The site does not encourage play for money and provides no information intended to improve outcomes in a money game.

Not legal, financial or medical advice

No content here should be read as professional advice of any kind. Nothing on the site creates a professional relationship.

Scoring disagreements

Scoring is the most disputed part of mahjong, and for good reason: different rule sets count the same hand differently, and some patterns are defined in more than one way. Pages describing scoring aim to state which rule set a value belongs to. If two sources disagree about a value, the difference is noted rather than hidden. The site accepts no responsibility for a disputed score at your table.
